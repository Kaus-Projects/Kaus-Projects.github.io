How the crew uses it

It's 4pm. The unit is down. The customer is watching.

What your techs see on the call.

  1. Step 1

    They tell it what they're looking at.

    Residential or commercial. AC split, heat pump, furnace, boiler, mini split, rooftop unit, walk-in, rack system, ice machine. Pick the symptom. The app only shows the ones that fit that unit.

  2. Step 2

    They enter the readings, or take a photo.

    Suction, head, superheat, subcool, indoor and outdoor temps. Or a photo of the nameplate or the gauges, and MasterTech reads the numbers.

  3. Step 3

    One question at a time until it's confirmed.

    Each step is one card: the single most useful test or question. Likely faults are ranked with a confidence score. Unsafe conditions stop the job. When it's confirmed, the customer summary and the work order land on your dashboard.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Do my techs pay?

No. Plans are billed to the company, per technician. Techs on your plan never pay. An independent tech without a company gets 5 free diagnostics a month, or 60 a month on the $9.99 Individual plan.

How do techs join my company?

Your Account and Team screens show a company invite code. A tech enters it at sign-up and appears on your roster. Regenerate the code any time; the old one stops working immediately.

Which equipment does it cover?

Residential: AC split, heat pump, package unit, mini split, furnace, boiler. Commercial: rooftop unit (RTU), walk-in, reach-in, rack system, ice machine, split system, VRF. Refrigerants from R-410A, R-32 and R-454B to R-448A, R-404A and R-290.

Does it replace the technician?

No. It can't hold the gauges. It's a second set of eyes: it checks their numbers against the physics, tells them when a reading contradicts the theory, and flags unsafe conditions. The tech confirms the diagnosis and closes the call.

What about our data?

Readings, photos and work orders are stored in your team's accounts. Contractors see their own team only. Only techs who mark themselves open to work appear in Find Techs. Delete your account from inside the app any time. Details in the Privacy Policy.
