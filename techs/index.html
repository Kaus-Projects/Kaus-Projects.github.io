How it works

It's 4pm. The unit is down. The customer is watching.

From broken unit to confirmed diagnosis in three steps.

  1. Step 1

    Tell it what you're looking at.

    Residential or commercial. AC split, heat pump, furnace, boiler, mini split, rooftop unit, walk-in, rack system, ice machine. Pick the symptom. The app only shows the ones that fit that unit.

  2. Step 2

    Enter the readings, or just take a photo.

    Suction, head, superheat, subcool, indoor and outdoor temps. Or snap the nameplate or your gauges and MasterTech reads the numbers for you.

  3. Step 3

    Answer one question at a time until it's confirmed.

    Each step is one card: the single most useful test or question. Likely faults are ranked with a confidence score. Unsafe conditions stop the job. When it's confirmed, you get a customer-ready summary and a saved work order.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is it a chatbot?

Not a chat box. Each step is one card: a question with tappable answers, a reading to enter, a test to run, or a photo to take. The engine ranks the faults and picks the next most useful step. Free text is the last resort.

Which equipment does it cover?

Residential: AC split, heat pump, package unit, mini split, furnace, boiler. Commercial: rooftop unit (RTU), walk-in, reach-in, rack system, ice machine, split system, VRF. Refrigerants from R-410A, R-32 and R-454B to R-448A, R-404A and R-290.

Does it replace the technician?

No. It can't hold the gauges. It's a second set of eyes: it checks your numbers against the physics, tells you when a reading contradicts your theory, and flags unsafe conditions. The tech confirms the diagnosis and closes the call.

Do I pay if my company has a plan?

No. Plans are billed to the company, per technician. Join with your company's invite code at sign-up and your diagnostics are covered by its plan.

What about my data?

Your readings, photos and work orders are stored in your account. Contractors see their own team only. Only techs who mark themselves open to work appear in recruiter search. Delete your account from inside the app any time. Details in the Privacy Policy.
